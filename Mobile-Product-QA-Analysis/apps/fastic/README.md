# Fastic — Product & QA Analysis

A hands-on analysis of the Fastic intermittent-fasting app, evaluated on a real
device (iPhone 13, iOS) by going through the full first-time user experience.

The goal is to look at the app through both a **product** and a **quality** lens:
not only where it works or breaks technically, but where it succeeds or fails at
delivering real value to the user and the business.

## Contents

- **[Product-Analysis.md](Product-Analysis.md)** — Full product and UX analysis:
  FTUE funnel, UI evaluation, ethical review, prioritized recommendations, and
  product edge cases, with annotated screenshots.
- **screenshots/** — Evidence captured during the onboarding walkthrough.

## Core finding

Fastic sells the *promise* of personalization but does not *deliver* it: the app
asks 39 onboarding questions yet shows almost no real, personal value before a
trial-free paywall. The full reasoning, evidence, and recommendations are in
[Product-Analysis.md](Product-Analysis.md).
