# Wise: Product & QA Analysis

A short product and quality analysis of the Wise app, evaluated on a real device
(iPhone 13, iOS) as a new user in Turkey, focused on the onboarding and transfer
experience of an international money-transfer product.

---

> ### Executive Summary
>
> - **Honest, focused onboarding:** Wise moves the user quickly and securely to the
>   one feature available in Turkey (international transfer), labeling unavailable
>   features "not available yet" rather than over-promising.
> - **Retention risk:** With only a single feature available in Turkey, a user may not
>   return after one transfer. Wise appears to mitigate this with a referral program.
> - **Trust risk (fee visibility):** The transfer fee is disclosed but visually
>   understated, which sits in an ethical grey area against Wise's transparency brand.
> - **Security-first design:** Passcode, Face ID, and 2-step verification are required,
>   and disabling security notifications triggers a warning.

## Scope

This analysis is limited to what is observable without completing real transactions or
full KYC, on iOS, for a user in Turkey. It is a focused case-style analysis rather than
an exhaustive test effort.
