# Mobile Product & QA Analysis

Hands-on analyses of real mobile apps, looked at through both a **product** and a
**quality** lens: not only where an app works or breaks technically, but where it
succeeds or fails at delivering real value to the user and the business.

Each analysis is based on actual use on a real device (full first-time user
experience, edge-case inputs, real screenshots) rather than assumptions.

## Apps

- **[Fastic](apps/fastic/)**: in-depth product and QA analysis of an
  intermittent-fasting app. Full package: product and UX analysis, QA perspective,
  test cases, bug reports with evidence, and risk analysis.
- **[Wise](apps/wise/)**: a focused, case-style analysis of a fintech money-transfer
  app, covering onboarding, security, and transfer flow, with product and UX analysis,
  test cases, and a QA perspective.

The two are deliberately different: Fastic shows depth across a full QA package, and
Wise shows a focused analysis of a different domain (fintech). This is an ongoing
portfolio that grows one app at a time using the same approach.

## Approach

Every analysis aims to:

- identify a single core problem and organize findings around it, rather than
  listing unrelated issues;
- separate observation (what was seen on the device) from hypothesis (what it likely
  causes), and note how each hypothesis could be validated;
- tie product and UX findings to concrete business outcomes (conversion, retention,
  churn, liability);
- give balanced credit for what an app does well, not only what it does poorly.
